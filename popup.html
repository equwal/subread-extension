SubRead: your subtitles on YouTube

  1. Open a video on YouTube, for example an audiobook.
  2. Press SR at the top right of the player.
  3. Choose the .srt of that recording. If the video has an intro, play to the first line and press First line is now.
  4. Check Transcript to also show your subtitles as a transcript panel next to the video.

No subtitles yet? subread.space makes them from the audiobook and its ebook, free, in your browser. The words come from the book, so names and spelling are right.

The extension does not read, record or download the video. Your subtitles stay in this browser. Open source: GitHub. Free, no ads: support it on Ko-fi.
